## 2. Camera heading reset to a garbage yaw after mode 17 — "random turn"

`camera.cpp:291`: after more than 500 ms without a QuasiFPS frame (mode 17 lasted 0.6–1.1 s), `g_yaw = gameYaw`.
`gameYaw` is taken from the first frame back, i.e. from the far mode-17 eye toward the target — an arbitrary
heading. Example 23:12:11 → 23:12:13: camera yaw before 1.345, Leon 1.287, after the reset **2.641 (+74°)**.

Knock-on: an aim started 0.3 s later locked onto the point the (wrong) free camera looked at:
`23:12:13.658 cam: aim point lock done, view turned +46.2 deg sideways`.

## 3. Aim point lock spins on the boat (Del Lago, r_no_1 0x0F) — "random turn while aiming"

17 aims between 22:46 and 22:58 logged accumulated lock turns of **±185° to ±2491°** within 0.3 s
(`22:55:24.091 view turned +2491.2 deg sideways`); on foot they are 0–20°. Over that one aim the view went from
yaw 0.479 to 2.068 (**+91°**).

Cause: the lock keeps looking at a fixed **world** point (`g_aimP`) while the boat moves Leon at ~13 000 u/s, so the
point drops behind within frames. The game (boat routine) also rewrites Leon's yaw every frame, so the full
difference is re-applied each frame and added to `g_aimYawOff` / `g_aimPitch0` (camera.cpp, lock block) — that
also corrupts the free camera heading handed back at aim end.
